Jarvis is a voice-first assistant I built for my own college life. It runs my calendar, calls me in the morning with a briefing, tracks my goals, money and food, sorts my college email, and rings my phone when something urgent comes in.

What it does

One assistant for the whole day

Everything works by voice: ask, and Jarvis uses its tools and shows what it's doing on screen as it talks.

☀️

Morning briefing call

Rings at 7:30 like a phone call: what I covered yesterday, today's classes, to-dos, goals and files to go through.

📅

Calendar & to-dos

Google Calendar and Tasks by voice. Changes appear live in the real calendar app, with Jarvis floating on top.

☎️

It calls you

"Call me at 4 about the lab report." A real ringing call screen; works even with the app closed.

🔥

Goals & streaks

Habits with weekly targets, check-ins and streaks, plus an evening recap call about the day.

₹

Money

Payments are logged from bank emails, sorted into needs and wants. A notification after each one asks what it was for.

🍛

Food & calories

Snap your plate: it names the dishes (using the mess menu) and estimates calories and protein.

✉️

College mail

Sorts the college inbox into deadlines, class changes, events and exams, and rings for anything urgent.

📑

Slides walkthrough

Upload lecture slides or PDFs and Jarvis explains them slide by slide, out loud.

🧠

Memory

Remembers lasting facts (routines, people, preferences) and uses them across phone and laptop.

Agentic AI

A team of agents, not one big prompt

A small router decides which skills each message needs, so the main agent only sees the right tools. Background agents work on their own schedule and can reach the phone through push.

Router

A fast small model reads each message and picks the skill areas it needs, cutting each step from ~3.8K to ~1–2K tokens.

Jarvis (main agent)

Plans and calls tools in a loop, can unlock more tools itself, and needs your explicit yes before changing the calendar.

Briefing agent

Prepares the morning call ten minutes early from yesterday's journal, goals, spending and today's schedule.

Mail agent

Sorts new college email every 15 minutes and calls about urgent things (polite hours only).

Finance agent

Turns payment emails into a spending log, sorts need vs want, and notifies after each payment.

Food agent

A vision model reads meal photos and mess-menu screenshots, estimating calories and protein.

Tech stack

Built to cost nothing to run: free AI tiers, open-source voices and the laptop as the server.

Backend

  • Python · FastAPI
  • LangGraph agents
  • Groq (gpt-oss, Qwen vision, Whisper)
  • SQLite · local embeddings

App

  • Expo / React Native (Android + web)
  • Custom Kotlin module: calls, picture-in-picture, Clock app
  • Push via Expo + Firebase

Integrations

  • Google Calendar, Tasks, Gmail
  • ElevenLabs + Microsoft neural voices
  • Tailscale (phone ↔ laptop anywhere)
