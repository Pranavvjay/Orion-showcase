Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

In short: Orion is a personal assistant built and used by one person, its developer. It is not offered to the public. Data it reads from Google stays on the developer's own computer, is used only to provide the assistant's features to that same person, and is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Who this covers

Orion ("the app") is a private project made by Pranav (github.com/Pranavvjay). The only user is the developer, signing in with their own Google accounts. This policy explains what the app accesses and why, as required for its Google sign-in.

Google data the app accesses

PermissionWhat it's used for
Google CalendarReading the day's events for briefings and answering questions; adding, moving or deleting events only when the user explicitly asks and confirms.
Google TasksListing to-dos, adding them and marking them done at the user's request.
Gmail (main account)Reading payment-receipt emails from the user's payment app only, to keep a spending log, and moving those receipts to Trash once logged. No other email is searched or read.
Gmail (college account, read-only)Reading new college emails to sort them into deadlines, class changes, events and exams, and to answer questions about them. Nothing in this account is changed, sent or deleted.

Where data goes

  • Stored locally. The app's server runs on the developer's own laptop. Calendar results, sorted email summaries, the spending and food logs, goals, notes and memories are kept in local files on that laptop and are not uploaded to any database or cloud storage.
  • AI processing. To understand requests and write replies, the relevant text (and voice recordings or meal photos the user sends) is sent to Groq, an AI inference provider, and only for that request.
  • Speech. Orion's spoken replies are created by sending the reply text to a text-to-speech service (Microsoft's neural voices or ElevenLabs).
  • Notifications. To ring or notify the user's own phone, short messages (for example "₹70 to a shop") are delivered through Expo's push service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Connection. The phone reaches the laptop over a private, encrypted Tailscale network.

No data is sold, rented, or shared with anyone for advertising, analytics or any other purpose, and none of it is used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Orion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing features described above, is not transferred to others except as needed to provide those features (the processing services listed above), and is not read by humans.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on the laptop until the user deletes it. Access to Google can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; deleting the app's local data folder removes everything it stored.

Changes and contact
